Intended use

Purpose: A safe, simulated-data training view for understanding the relationships between artifacts in a SOC case — who was involved, what host, what file/email, what process spawned what, what destination was contacted, what alert fired, and what response followed.

Demonstrates: investigation reasoning — connecting evidence into an attack-and-response story.

Try: click any node for its details, or any connection for the supporting evidence; hover to highlight a path.

Does not: connect to real systems or use real data — every artifact shown is fictional.

Educational prototype. Uses simulated data only. Validate all logic before operational use.

How to Play

Answer questions → earn credits → build defenses → stop attackers.

1

Your mission

Protect the Domain Controller — the critical system at the end of the network path.

2

The attacker

Red-team attackers start at the Internet and move through the network toward the Domain Controller.

3

Earn credits

Answer cybersecurity questions correctly to earn credits. Use credits to buy defenses. Wrong answers do not give credits and may help the attacker advance.

4

Build defenses

Place blue-team defenses on build slots near the path. Defenses attack or slow enemies.

5

Start the wave

Click “Start Defense Wave” to launch an attack. Watch whether your defenses stop it.

6

If attackers get through

If attackers reach the Domain Controller, integrity drops. At zero integrity, the mission fails.

7

SOC Dashboard mode

SOC Dashboard is a timed mode — full explanations are not shown under pressure. If the After-Action Report is enabled (default ON), every missed question is replayed after the shift with the correct answer, the explanation, and the topic to review — like a real post-incident analyst learning report. The response timer is configurable (10–45 seconds, default 20s, or “No timer”): choose “No timer” to learn and review at your own pace, or a shorter timer to simulate real incident-response pressure.

★

Remember

Game rules are fixed. Correct answers earn rewards. Wrong answers help the attacker.
